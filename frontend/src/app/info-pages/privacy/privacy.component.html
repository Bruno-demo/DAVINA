Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

Davina Beauty GmbH, Schönhauser Allee 123, 10435 Berlin, Germany, is the data controller responsible for processing your personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following data when you use our services:

  • Account data: Name, email address, password (encrypted)
  • Order data: Shipping address, payment method, order history
  • Skin analysis data: Skin type quiz responses, uploaded photos (processed server-side, not shared)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, products viewed (for analytics and recommendations)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Providing personalised beauty and product recommendations
  • Sending order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Newsletter (only with your explicit consent)
  • Improving our products and services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Payment providers (Paystack) — to process payments securely
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your orders
  • Email service — to send transactional emails

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies. Your JWT token is stored in localStorage to keep you logged in.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for newsletter at any time

To exercise any of these rights, visit your Profile page or contact us.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, CSRF protection, XSS sanitisation, and rate limiting to protect your data.

8. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, email us at privacy@davina-beauty.com or contact us.
